    How to play

    Fill in the missing links in as few strokes as you can. Every word goes with the word after it to make a well-known phrase or compound word:

    PEANUT
    BUTTER
    C…
    C…
    P…
    STAR

    Peanut butter, buttercup, cupcake, cake pop, pop star.

    The rules

    • There is one new hole every day, the same for everyone. It changes at midnight UTC.
    • The first and last words are shown. For each word in between you only get its first letter, and you are not told how long it is.
    • Tap any missing word to choose it, type your guess and press Enter. Solve them in any order.
    • Every guess is one stroke, right or wrong.
    • A wrong guess reveals the next letter of that word.

    Scoring

    Par is two more than the number of missing words, so you can afford two wrong guesses. Lower is better.

    Eagle2 under par (no wrong guesses)
    Birdie1 under par
    ParPar
    Bogey1 over par
    Double Bogey2 over par
    Triple Bogey3 over par
    Quadruple Bogey4 over par
    XOut of strokes

    You get four strokes more than par. If you run out, the answers are revealed and the hole is scored as an X, which counts as 5 over par.

    Scorecard, hints and archive

    • Your scorecard runs Monday to Sunday and adds your daily scores into a week total such as +3 or -2. Missed days are not counted.
    • Once per hole you can watch a short ad for a hint: the next letter of the selected word, without using a stroke. Hints show as 💡 when you share.
    • The archive has past holes. Each is unlocked by watching a short ad, and does not affect your stats, streak or scorecard.
