Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

This policy explains what information ("the game", "we") handles when you play.

What we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address or any account. The game has no sign-up and no server of its own that stores information about you.

Information saved on your device

The game saves your progress in your browser's local storage so it is still there when you come back. This includes your guesses on each hole, your results, your statistics and streaks, and which archive holes you have unlocked. This information stays on your device and is never sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. Doing so resets your stats.

Hosting

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests the pages. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

Advertising

We plan to show ads provided by Google AdSense. When ads are shown:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info.

You can learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
