How to Play

is a word ladder with a hidden target, scored like golf. Get from the starting word to the secret target word in as few moves as you can.

The rules

  • Every day there is one new hole, with a 4-letter starting word and a hidden 4-letter target word. Everyone in the world gets the same hole.
  • On each move, change exactly one letter of your current word to make a new word.
  • The new word must be a real English word, and you cannot reuse a word already in your ladder.
  • You win when you land on the target word.

The colours

After every move, the tiles show how close your word is to the target. The starting word shows its colours right away.

  • Green: the letter is in the target, in this spot.
  • Yellow: the letter is in the target, but in a different spot.
  • Gray: the letter is not in the target.

If your word has the same letter twice, each copy is coloured only if the target has enough copies of that letter.

Scoring

Each hole has a par, shown at the top of the board. Par is two more than the fewest moves the hole can be solved in. Your score is your number of moves compared with par, and lower is better.

You get four moves more than par. If you use them all without reaching the target, the target is revealed and the hole is scored as an X, which counts as 5 over par.

Weekly scorecard

Your scorecard runs Monday to Sunday and adds up your daily scores into a week total such as +3 or -2. Days you miss are marked with a dash and are not counted. A new hole starts at midnight UTC.

Hints

Stuck? Once per hole you can watch a short ad to reveal one letter of the target in its correct spot. Hints are marked with 💡 when you share your result.

Practice and archive

Practice mode gives you unlimited random holes. The archive lets you play past holes. Neither one affects your stats, streak or scorecard.
